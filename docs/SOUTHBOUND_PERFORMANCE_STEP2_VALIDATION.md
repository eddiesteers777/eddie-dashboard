## 2. How the screens were inspected

The real site code was rendered in Chromium (Playwright) against local Firebase **Auth + Firestore emulators running the repo's real `firestore.rules`**. Only `js/firebase.js` was swapped at serve time (to point at the emulators and sign in a test user); every other page, script and stylesheet ran unchanged.

Test accounts (made up, not real data):

| Persona | Account | What it has |
|---|---|---|
| Coach | "Eddie Steers" (approved coach) | Built-in marathon plan, 10 weeks of fake COROS runs, 40 nights of fake sleep/HRV |
| Training client | "Sam Rivera" (online coaching + running + strength) | A published coach plan generated by the real generator (half marathon, week 3 of 11), profile, a check-in, an unread message, an update |
| Soccer client | "Jo Park" (soccer 1-on-1) | Weekly booked session |

Coverage: 52 page renders at 390px across the four personas; Today, My Plan, Progress, More, Coach dashboard and Client Hub also at 320, 375, 430, 768, 1024, 1440; a training day (Thursday) and a rest day (Wednesday). Each render recorded horizontal overflow, page errors, the headings and buttons in the first screen, tap targets under 40px, text under 12px and the page height.

**Not covered** (stated so nobody assumes otherwise):

- Interactive flows: strength workout mode / set runner, fueling result, the coach plan editor, dialogs and bottom sheets, messages composing.
- The client Readiness card (it only appears with COROS connected on that device).

## 3. What's already right (keep it)

- **No horizontal overflow** on any page at any width from 320 to 1440, and **no page errors** in any render.
- **Access control holds.** Jo (soccer only) opening `plan.html` and Pat (pending) opening `running.html` were both sent back to Today. The dock, menus and More list only show each account's pages.
- **The client's Today is already coaching-led.** On a training day it opens with the workout ("Workout · 4 mi · Mile repeats…") and its actions, then NEXT, then From Your Coach, then the week. That's the hierarchy the blueprint asks for.
- **The coach dashboard** (`coach.html`) leads with a one-line summary and "Who needs you today" grouped by task. Good hierarchy, nothing to restructure.
- **The workout page** is clear: date/week/phase, distance, why, coach cue, warm-up / main set / cool-down, fuel.

## 5. The concept images vs. what the app can honestly show

The three generated concepts are a good read on mood (dark forest, cream type, sand action, photo atmosphere, a floating dock). Several elements in them have **no data or feature behind them** and shouldn't be built as drawn:

| Concept element | Reality | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Community** tab and posts | No community feature exists. | Drop. Building one is a product decision, not a redesign. |
| Weather + city ("72°, Birmingham, AL") | No weather source; it would need a weather API and location permission. | Drop. |
| Body Battery, "Fatigue 7.6", Readiness "8.2" | Garmin terms. COROS gives HRV, resting HR, sleep, recovery %. Southbound's readiness is 0–100. | Use the real parts (Sleep, Readiness, HRV rings already exist). |
| "Training Load 87% · Optimal" | The model deliberately never calls load "safe" or "optimal" (load percentile is observational only). | Show the real wording ("higher than 80% of your year"), never a verdict. |
| Mountain-peak logo | The brand is the SB speed mark. | Keep the real mark. |
| Full-bleed runner photo behind Today | App pages have no photos; the public site uses stock. A photo adds ~100–200 KB to first loads and to the offline precache. | Eddie's call (6.4). Recommended: no photo on phones; at most a subtle one on desktop Today. |
| 4-up stats with % changes | The coach has watch data; most clients have only logged runs and see zeros. | Show a stat only when there's data behind it. |
| Fueling ring (72 g of 90 g) | Nutrition goals and logs exist (`nutritionSnap` on Today). | Feasible as a compact Today row. |
| Upcoming race with progress bar | Race date, countdown and week N of M all exist. | Feasible. |
| Recent activity table with effort chips | Train → Recent Workouts already exists; effort exists for rated runs. | Keep on Train; at most a short "last run" line on Today. |

## 6. Decisions for Eddie

**Decided 2026-10-14 (Eddie chose the recommended option on all five):** dock = Today · Plan · Train · **Fuel** · More for training clients, Today · Coach · Train · Fuel · More for the coach, soccer-only unchanged, pending Today · More (Habits gets a check-off row on Today; "Health" is renamed "Fuel"); the plan tab keeps the name "Plan"; workout cards: Start is the main button before the workout, Mark done after; no photos in the app; the coach's Today shows today's run first with readiness as a strip under it.

**Done:** step 1 (phone header budget), step 2 (coach Today: run first, readiness strip), 2026-10-14.

1. **Phone dock.** The blueprint proposes Today · My Week · Progress · More for everyone. Evidence pushes back: Train (strength workouts, Log a run) and Health (meals logged several times a day) are daily-use, while My Progress is a four-week summary that's useful weekly at most. Putting Progress in the dock and Train/Health in More adds a tap to the most frequent actions.
   - **Recommended:** a maximum of 5, based on services. Training client: **Today · Plan · Train · Fuel · More**. Coach: **Today · Coach · Train · Fuel · More**. Soccer only: **Today · Sessions · Habits · More** (unchanged). Pending: **Today · More**. Habits gets an inline "Today's habits" check-off row on Today (one tap, as now) and stays in More; Progress is linked from Today's stats row ("See your progress", already there) and sits first in More.
   - **Alternative (blueprint as written):** Today · Plan · Progress · More. It's simpler, but Train, Fuel and Habits each cost an extra tap.
2. **Label for the plan tab:** keep "Plan" (the page is My Plan and shows a week at a time) or rename to "Week".
3. **Workout card primary action:** Start before the workout, Mark done after (recommended), or leave Mark done primary.
4. **Photo atmosphere:** none in the app (recommended), desktop-only, or everywhere.
5. **Coach Today order:** today's run first with a compact readiness strip under it (recommended), or keep Readiness first.

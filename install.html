Open this page in Safari first You're in another app's browser, which can't add apps to your Home Screen. Tap ••• (or the Share button) and choose "Open in Safari", then follow these steps.
Not in Safari? In Chrome, Share is at the right end of the address bar. If "Add to Home Screen" isn't in the list, open this page in Safari.

Read all three steps first: Safari's Share menu covers this page.

Step 1 of 3

  1. 1Tap the Share button

    The square with an arrow pointing up , in Safari's toolbar at the bottom of the screen (at the top on an iPad).

    Don't see it? Tap ••• next to the address first, then Share.

  2. 2Find "Add to Home Screen"

    Scroll down the list that opens. It's usually below Copy and Add to Favorites.

    Not in the list? Tap More or Edit Actions at the bottom, then turn on Add to Home Screen.

  3. 3Tap "Add to Home Screen", then "Add"

    "Add" is in the top-right corner. Southbound appears on your Home Screen: open it from there and sign in.

In short Share → Add to Home Screen → Add

Your phone asks you to confirm, then Southbound is on your home screen.

Install it from your browser's menu:

Step 1 of 3

  1. 1Tap the ⋮ menu

    Three dots in the top-right corner of Chrome.

    Samsung Internet: tap ≡ at the bottom, then "Add page to".

  2. 2Tap "Install app"

    Some phones call it "Add to Home screen" instead.

  3. 3Tap "Install"

    Southbound appears on your home screen and in your apps: open it from there and sign in.

In short ⋮ menu → Install app → Install

Southbound opens in its own window, like any other app.

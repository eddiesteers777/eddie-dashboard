Adds your whole Strava history to the charts above. Runs COROS already has are recognized and counted once. Your phone or computer reads the archive itself: only a summary of each activity is saved to your account, never your routes.

No Strava history imported yet.

How to get your Strava archive
  1. On a computer, go to strava.com and sign in.
  2. Click your picture (top right), then Settings, then My Account.
  3. Under "Download or Delete Your Account", click Get Started.
  4. Under "Download Request", click Request Your Archive. Strava emails you a link (it can take a few hours).
  5. Download the .zip from that email, then come back here and click Choose the Strava .zip. Already unzipped it? Choose the folder instead.

Importing again later is safe: activities already here are updated, not doubled.

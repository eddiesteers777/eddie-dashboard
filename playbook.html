Southbound Playbook

Draw the play, plan the practice, keep the roster, take attendance. A free set of tools for soccer coaches, built for 11v11 youth and school teams. No account needed. Sign in only if you want it on every device.

Open the tactical board Plan a practice
01 · Board

Tactical board

Drag players, draw passes, runs and zones, switch formations, then save, print or export an image. Works with a mouse, a finger or a pen.

 02 · Drills

Drill library

60+ starter drills with setup, rotations, coaching points, a harder and an easier version. Add your own and attach a board diagram.

 03 · Practice

Practice planner

Tell it your players, time, equipment and focus. Get a timed plan you can edit, then print it on one page.

 04 · Roster

Roster and lineup

Keep first names and numbers, mark who is here today, and fill your formation by position in one tap.

 05 · Team

Attendance and duty

Take attendance, see who owes a make-up run, and rotate who carries the equipment at practices and games.

 06 · Game day

Lineup and subs

Fair lineups for every half or quarter, a substitution plan, minutes played for each player, and a card to print for the sideline.
Your data

Yours to keep, on every device

With no account, the Playbook saves your boards, drills, practices and roster in this browser on this device and uploads nothing. Sign in with Google and the same work is also saved to your private Southbound account, so it follows you to your phone, tablet and computer. Nobody else can open it. Use first names only.

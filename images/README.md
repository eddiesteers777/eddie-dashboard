# Site photos

Upload photos into this folder with these exact filenames (lowercase, `.jpg`). Each one shows up on the site automatically; until then, that spot shows a styled placeholder.

| Filename | Where it appears | Best shape |
|---|---|---|
| `eddie-hero.jpg` | Home, top of page, next to the headline | Portrait (4:5), you coaching or training |
| `eddie-portrait.jpg` | Home, "Meet Your Coach" | Square, a clear headshot or upper-body shot |
| `offer-online-coaching.jpg` | Home, Online Coaching card | Landscape (16:10) |
| `offer-running-strength.jpg` | Home, Running & Strength card | Landscape (16:10) |
| `offer-soccer-1on1.jpg` | Home, 1-on-1 Soccer card | Landscape (16:10) |
| `offer-soccer-group.jpg` | Home, Group Soccer card | Landscape (16:10) |
| `eddie-about.jpg` | About, next to your bio | Portrait (4:5) |
| `gallery-1.jpg`, `gallery-2.jpg`, `gallery-3.jpg` | About, photo row under your bio | Square |

Photos are cropped to fit automatically, so keep the subject near the center. Around 1600px on the long side is plenty. Phone photos are fine, but large originals slow the page down.

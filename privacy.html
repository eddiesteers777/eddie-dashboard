Privacy

Your information, in plain language.

What this site and app collect, where it lives, who can see it, and how to have it deleted.

What's collected

  • When you sign in: your name and email address from your Google account. Sign-in is handled by Google; this site never sees your password.
  • When you apply: the answers you give on the application form (goals, experience, availability and the services you're interested in).
  • When you use the app: the training you log (runs, workouts, habits, nutrition, check-ins), your plans, and any session requests you make.
  • When you ask a question: what you type into the Contact form (name, email and/or phone, topic, message, and an athlete's age if you share it). You don't need an account for this.

Where it's stored

Account and training data are stored with Google Firebase. The app also keeps a copy on your own device so it works offline. If you connect a COROS watch, the sign-in connection stays on your device; the workouts it brings in are saved with the rest of your training.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own data.
  • Your coach can see your profile, application, the plans you share, your session requests and your weekly check-ins, so they can coach you.
  • Nobody else. Your information is never sold, rented or used for advertising. There are no ad trackers on this site.

Emails

The app sends a few emails: a short "your coach replied" note when a session request or check-in is answered, and alerts to the coach when you apply, book or check in. These are sent with EmailJS. There is no mailing list and no marketing email.

Young athletes

Soccer training is open to young players, but accounts and questions for anyone under 13 should come from a parent or guardian.

Deleting your information

You can ask for your account and everything in it to be deleted at any time. Send a request through the Contact page and it will be taken care of. You can also clear the copy on your device by signing out and removing the app.
